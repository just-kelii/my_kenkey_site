Know Your Kenkey

Ga kenkey
Fermented corn dough, wrapped in corn husk, with a slightly sour taste.
Fante kenkey
Wrapped in plantain leaf, softer and less sour than Ga kenkey.
Shito
Spicy black pepper sauce made with dried fish and shrimp.
